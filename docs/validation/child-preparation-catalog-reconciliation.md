# Format-6 catalog and original preparation effect reconciliation

Tracking: [#1423](https://github.com/Rambolarsen/orkworks/issues/1423),
[#1176](https://github.com/Rambolarsen/orkworks/issues/1176). Inspection base:

## Result and limits

The selected contracts name exactly **35 distinct logical families**: the already
[enumerated 33-family inventory](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-02-hierarchy-storage-kernel.md#required-family-inventory),
plus accepted ordinary lineage and preparation authorization. This closes the
name/key inventory reconciliation for this selected profile. It does not close
every executable codec/command, physical index or native profile. The located
mechanical fixture contains 17 of those names and lacks 18; eight of its declared
families are explicitly outside its producer scope. Production admission refuses
the profile before I/O. Table presence is not source or transaction qualification.

The [representation acceptance](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-child-preparation-authorization-design.md#scoped-representation-acceptance--2026-11-05)
stands, along with the joint broker, retained lineage/tombstones and scoped
late-saving/no-spawn policy. This audit changes no codec, domain, limit, authority,
index layout or compatibility decision. RegistryRoot remains separately proposed.
No SQL/database/DDL, fixture, runtime registration or native/model probe ran.

## Commit links, retention and membership closure

Compute existing source/attachment digests, candidate authorization digest and
exact retained charges/row projections before C. Hash format6 change/row envelopes
with the accepted exclusions: the lineage's preparationCommit and authorization's
outer lastCommit are new-C links; authorization expectedCommit and embedded old
snapshot origin CommitRefs stay committed. Inherit all other exact row exclusions
from their owners. No extra self-link exclusion or hash fixed point is selected.

Each retained consumer protects its exact required source/origin/H/C proofs.
Historical control snapshots compare with their original manifest afterDigest,
not today's mutable control. Sparse direct-proof retention does not require every
unrelated predecessor forever. Deleted historical rows are not automatically
required to survive merely because a retained manifest once changed them.

The two added families are fixed typed metadata consumers. Ordinary lineage
resolves original P/source/attachment/creation identity/C; authorization resolves
its full four snapshots, source/parent/runtime/approval/reservation/allocation,
H/origin/C and ordinary lineage. These are not new ObjectRef kinds or dependency
purposes. The authorization's PlanControlRead does not silently extend the
separate recovery-decision ControlSnapshotTable union; its own accepted fixed
consumer recipe protects that original plan snapshot and proof.

A qualified physical representation must prove exact forward/reverse/direct
correspondence for these recipes, full canonical permit uniqueness, retained
child/session/attachment uniqueness, and complete absence at captured H/G,
including linked tombstones. Returned rows or a digest-only index do not prove
completion. Keep metadata discovery within 4,096 observations / 16 MiB cumulative
copies without fetching/hashing evaluation content before debit. Unknown or
unsupported consumers fence the operation. Cancellation/Delete/run completion
cannot prune evidence, reset ledgers or turn incomplete recovery into a new epoch.

## Concrete remaining deliverable

The logical name/key inventory and original-attach effect obligations are now
reconciled against the two accepted additions. The next missing design deliverable
is the **physical paired membership/index contract**: exact metadata-only forward,
reverse and direct lookup representation; full-key uniqueness including permit
and ordinary lineage; atomic owning maintenance; complete bounded absence and
sparse origin-proof recovery at one H/G. Reuse these logical recipes; do not start
another catalog-count or representation-approval task. New physical persistence/
compatibility choices require their own concrete scoped review before dependent
implementation. This audit selects none of them.

Actual ordinary/source/revocation owners and hard physical/native resource
qualification remain parallel prerequisites, not facts an index design can
supply. RegistryRoot is independently pending. Complete transaction fixtures and
any executable/native plan retain their existing gates. #1176 stays open, #740
stays closed research; no runtime activation follows from this audit.
